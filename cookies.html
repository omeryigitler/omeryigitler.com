Cookie Policy

Last Updated: February 12, 2026

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device to help websites function better. We use them to ensure security, understand user behavior, and improve your experience.

How we use cookies

On this website, we only use essential and analytical cookies. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies for third-party marketing.

Cookie Source Purpose Duration
__cf_bm Cloudflare Bot management and security. (Essential) 30 Minutes
_clck Microsoft Clarity Persists the Clarity User ID to improve analytics. 1 Year
_clsk Microsoft Clarity Connects multiple page views into a single session. 1 Day

Managing Cookies

You can choose to disable cookies through your browser settings. However, please note that some security features like Turnstile Verification may not function correctly if cookies are completely blocked.
